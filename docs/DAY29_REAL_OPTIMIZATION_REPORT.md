## 3. Problems found and fixed

| # | Severity | Problem | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | Critical | `PerceptualHash.of` returned `0` for every real JPEG: a scaled `Graphics2D.drawImage` of a color-managed (ICC, `TYPE_3BYTE_BGR`) image rendered a flat background. All candidate frames collapsed into one slide. | Scale with `getScaledInstance(..., SCALE_AREA_AVERAGING)` and compute luminance from RGB. Regression test `realJpegHashIsNotZero` added. |
| S2 | High | Slide dedup compared only to the **previous** kept hash, so a slide repeated later in the lecture survived as a duplicate. | Compare each candidate against **all** kept hashes. Regression test `SlideExtractionServiceTest` added. |
| S3 | High | Text generation and RAG shared one chunk size, forcing a trade-off between LLM-call count and retrieval granularity. | Decoupled: `llm-chunk-chars` (generation) vs new `rag-chunk-chars` (indexing). |
| S4 | Medium | Optimized prompts (`v2`) did not actually reduce output on the fixture corpus and lowered `clean` quality. | Measured honestly; kept as non-default variants. See §5. |

## 5. Prompt optimization

Versioned prompts introduced:
- `clean-transcript-v1` = v1-baseline; `clean-transcript-v2` = v2-conservative-clean
- `structured-notes-v1` = v1-baseline; `structured-notes-v2` = v3-optimized-notes

Measured effect: **no reduction** in output tokens on this corpus
(clean 303→304, notes 387→391); v2 `clean` was slightly worse. Kept as
available variants, not defaults. The dominant speed levers are the model and
thinking mode, not prompt verbosity, for this content length.

## 6. Chunking + concurrency

| maxChars | LLM calls | output tokens | sequential ms | concurrency=2 ms |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 300 | 5 | 841 | 19 958 | 9 187 |
| 1000 | 1 | 313 | 5 038 | 5 078 |
| 3000 | 1 | 318 | 5 069 | 4 972 |
| 6000 | 1 | 313 | 4 987 | 5 044 |

Conclusion: fewer, larger chunks win; concurrency=2 only helps when many chunks
exist and never beats a single large call here. **Sequential + large generation
chunks** is recommended; RAG keeps small chunks via `rag-chunk-chars`.

## 8. Recommended configuration

`qwen3:1.7b`, thinking disabled, `numCtx=4096`, `numPredict=1536`,
`temperature=0.1`, generation `llm-chunk-chars=6000/400`,
RAG `rag-chunk-chars=400/60`, sequential, v1 prompts.

Before/after (notes): qwen3:4b thinking ≈ 187 s → qwen3:1.7b thinking-off ≈ 5.8 s
(~32x faster) with equal key-fact preservation.

## 12. Remaining limitations

- dHash on a 9x8 thumbnail is weak for slides that differ only in text; distinct
  layouts/patterns are separated reliably, near-identical text slides may merge.
- Semantic slide↔section alignment remains time-based (chronological); no OCR.
- `qwen3:4b` cannot be used reliably for `clean` on 4 GB VRAM (budget/truncation).
- Quality rubric is agent-assessed; deterministic checks support but do not
  replace it.
